### Roadmap
- **B direction on the printer: closed 2026-11-06 (user's decision): the firmware stays as is, B+ goes towards the plate centre, `b_sign = +1`.** Nothing to change in the engine, the preview or the kernel; the convention is already written in the G-code header (`; apex_kinematics pivot_to_tip=46 b_sign=1 tilt_sign=1 z_reference=tip`, read by `utils/kinematics.ts`). Reopen only if the firmware is changed.
- **Nozzle profile system** (user, 2026-10-07; **done 2026-11-06 for the geometry**): each printer profile carries `apex.default_nozzle_profile.length_mm`, `angle_deg` and `outer_width_mm` (`null`/« Inconnu » = not measured; format in `docs/PRINTER_PROFILE_FORMAT.md`, ADR 0003). `utils/aeolisNozzle.ts` starts from the active printer's profile and keeps the user's edits per printer. Length and angle go to the engine; the outer width is informative until the engine has a parameter for it. Still to do: named nozzle profiles (`profile_id`) shared between printers, and measured values for Aetheris-4 (today 10° / 2 mm are the engine defaults, `outer_width_mm` unknown).
- **Finish cloning OrcaSlicer's auto-arrange and add the related slicer functions** (user, 2026-10-31). `utils/orcaArrange.ts` only clones part of it (credited in `utils/credits.ts`; AGPL-3.0 notice to keep). Read the real sources (`src/slic3r/GUI/Jobs/ArrangeJob.cpp`, `src/libslic3r/Arrange.cpp`, libnest2d's `nfpplacer.hpp` / `firstfit.hpp`) before cloning — the first pass was written from summaries, not from the code.
  - *Re-read from the real sources 2026-11-06* (`Arrange.cpp`, `nfpplacer.hpp`, `firstfit.hpp`; the sources are not kept in the repo, clone `OrcaSlicer/OrcaSlicer` sparsely: `src/libslic3r`, `src/slic3r/GUI/Jobs`, `deps_src/libnest2d`). `utils/orcaArrange.ts` now follows `AutoArranger::objfunc` (BIG / LAST_BIG / SMALL cases, neighbour-alignment term for same-area copies, R = remaining / total including fixed items), `first_object` (first piece in the top-right corner at the seed angle), fixed items, the bounding-circle fit of a round bed and the final CENTER alignment; `Align to Y axis` is the principal axis of the second moments (`update_selected_items_axis_align`, ignored when rotations are allowed, like in Orca). *Deliberate deviations*: NLopt L_SUBPLEX replaced by edge sampling + refinement; convex hulls instead of the merged non-convex pile. *Not cloned*: the sort keys other than area (bed temperature, extruder id, height in sequential mode); the incompatible-filament and mixed-colour rejection costs; sequential-print rules; exclusion zones; the wipe tower; the belt-printer path; non-circular beds; several plates with first-fit (an item that does not fit stays where it is, with a warning); the final alignment around fixed items (Orca re-centres the movable pile outside the fixed items' NFP; here the pile is not re-centred when a fixed item exists); the `accuracy` and `do_final_align` parameters.
  - *Related slicer functions*: **done 2026-11-06**: arrange only the selection (context menu), an « Auto-disposition » button in the toolbar (heroicons rectangle-group) whose menu holds Organiser, spacing (0 = auto), allow rotations, align to Y and arrange on import (texts in `utils/arrangeTooltips.ts`, supplied by the user, bottom bar only, no mouse bubble), locking a model (arrange leaves it alone and avoids it; locked/unselected models are the fixed items of the algorithm), progress after 400 ms and cancel with Échap (nothing moves before the end), undo of an arrange (one Ctrl+Z through `apex-arrange-finished`). *Still to do*: multiple plates and the « Plateau actif uniquement » option (Apex has a single plate), visible lock indicator on the model. (« Keep on plate » is not an Orca option: dropped.)
- Later, after more thought: live influence adjustment by shortcut/wheel; overhang indicator.
- Very long term: named history states.
